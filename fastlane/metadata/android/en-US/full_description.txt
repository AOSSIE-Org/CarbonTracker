CarbonTracker is a privacy-first app that tracks your fitness activity and the carbon savings of your daily movement.

Your data stays on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud tracking.

Features:
- Carbon dashboard showing your carbon savings
- Fitness metrics, with Health Connect support
- Map screen with location search
- Profile screen for your personal details
- Local-first storage on your device

CarbonTracker is free software, licensed under GPLv3, and developed with AOSSIE.
